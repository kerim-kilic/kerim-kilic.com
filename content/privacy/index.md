The short version: this site doesn't collect anything about you.

## What the site does

- It sets **no cookies** and runs **no analytics, advertising or tracking**.
- It loads **no third-party scripts**. The fonts and icons are served from this site itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other font or tracking service while you read a page.
- It has **no forms** and nothing to sign up for.

## When you visit

The pages are served by Amazon CloudFront (AWS), and the domain's DNS is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process your IP address to deliver the pages. I haven't switched on access logging, so I don't keep a record of who visited or what they read. AWS and Cloudflare handle the data they process under their own privacy terms.

## If you email me

I use your email address and your message only to reply to you. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I don't share your details.

## Links to other sites

The links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Credly lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
